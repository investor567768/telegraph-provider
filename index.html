How it stays honest

Every answer names the operator, the exact interval it covers and the operator's own file, so it can be checked by hand. Operators stamp their times differently (NYISO marks the end of each interval, MISO uses EST all year, CAISO uses GMT), and the miner converts each one to an exact UTC interval. Places it doesn't cover, such as ERCOT in Texas, are refused rather than matched to a similar-sounding name. A scheduled job compares its answers against the operators' published files every day and posts the results publicly.
